myna. — logo concepts

Myna in Telugu is a bird known for its voice and its intelligence. The real common myna has a yellow beak and a yellow patch around the eye, so Bento lime becomes the beak and eye in every concept. All are flat geometry (circles, one triangle, rounded bars): they stay sharp at 18 px and work on white, black and lime. Hover a big mark to see how it would “sing” as the app's loading or now-playing animation.
