MYNA – 6 more design languages

Same app, same features, six very different looks. Each shows Home and Now Playing with your real album covers. The two earlier options are Glass (mockup.html) and Brutalist (mockup-brutalist.html), so there are 8 to choose from in total.

STYLE 1

Swiss

The 1960s International Typographic Style: white page, black Helvetica, one red, a strict grid and thin rules. Calm, exact, timeless.

  • Huge tight headings, tiny labels
  • 1 px black rules split everything
  • Red marks only the active thing
Feels like: a Braun radio or a museum poster. Very quiet; lets the covers do the shouting.

STYLE 2

Vinyl (skeuomorphic)

Real objects: a wooden record shelf, paper track cards, brushed-metal buttons and a turntable that spins the album while it plays.

  • Albums are record sleeves you pull off a shelf
  • Now Playing is a turntable; the cover is the record label
  • The crossfade is a real DJ fader knob
Feels like: your dad's hi-fi cabinet. Warm and nostalgic, with the most "wow" on first open.

STYLE 3

Y2K Neon

The year-2000 future: chrome lettering, pink-to-cyan neon, glossy pill buttons, sparkles and a spinning rainbow ring around the cover.

  • Chrome text, glossy jelly buttons
  • Equaliser bars bounce with the music
  • Faint scanlines over everything
Feels like: Winamp skins and an iPod nano ad. Loud, fun, very Gen-Z right now.

STYLE 4

Clay

Soft, squishy 3D shapes in pastel peach, lilac and mint, like modelling clay. Big rounded buttons that look pressable.

  • Puffy buttons with inner light and shadow
  • The cover gently bobs while playing
  • Very friendly and touch-first
Feels like: a Nintendo menu or a kids' toy. Warm, cute, least "serious" option.

STYLE 5

Editorial magazine

A music magazine on cream paper: serif headlines in italics, a masthead, drop caps, columns, and captions under photos. Lyrics read like pull-quotes.

  • Home is a front page with a "lead story"
  • Now Playing shows the current lyric as a quote
  • Deep red ink for accents
Feels like: Rolling Stone or The New Yorker. Grown-up and elegant; great with lyrics.

STYLE 6

Bento

Everything is a rounded tile in a grid, like a Japanese lunch box or Apple's keynote slides. Light grey, white tiles, black, and an electric lime accent.

  • Home mixes covers, stats and shortcuts in tiles
  • Now Playing is also a grid of tiles
  • Lime marks the crossfade zone and Play
Feels like: a modern widget dashboard. Clean, current and easy to extend with new tiles.
